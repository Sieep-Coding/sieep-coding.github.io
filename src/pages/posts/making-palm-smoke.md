I made this using [FL Studio!](/posts/switching-back-to-fl-studio)

After finishing [*Side Streets*](/posts/making-side-streets), I wanted to make something a little slower. I've been listening to a lot of old [reggae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reggae) lately, and I liked how much space those records leave between the instruments.

## The Groove

This beat really started with the drums.

The hi-hats have more swing than I usually use, and I spent a while moving kicks and snares around until everything felt like it was rolling instead of marching. Once I found the pocket, everything else came together pretty naturally.

I also kept the percussion simple. A few extra sounds go a long way when they're in the right place.

## The Melody

Most of the melodic ideas were inspired by old [dub](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dub_music) records and the warm, slightly imperfect sound of [analog recording](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Analog_recording). I wanted everything to feel a little dusty without sounding low quality.

Instead of constantly introducing new instruments, I reused the same ideas in different ways throughout the beat. Small changes usually end up being more interesting than big ones.

## Looking Forward

I think this is the closest I've gotten to making something that sounds like me.

There are influences from boom bap, reggae, and even a little [jazz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jazz), but it doesn't really fit into any one category. I'm starting to realize that's probably a good thing.

As much as I like experimenting, I keep coming back to beats that leave room to breathe. I think that's the direction I want to keep exploring.
